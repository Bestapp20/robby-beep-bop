Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

Robby Beep Bop Uploader ("the app") is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Robby Beep Bop.

What the app accesses

The app uses YouTube Data API scopes solely to upload, schedule, and manage videos and playlists on the owner's own YouTube channel. It does not access any other user's account.

Data storage

OAuth tokens are stored only locally on the owner's machine and are never transmitted to any third party.

Data collection and sharing

The app does not collect, share, or sell any personal data. It does not collect any data from children, viewers, or other users of YouTube.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
